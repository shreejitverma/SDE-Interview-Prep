# Behavioral Interview Guide

## The STAR Method
Answer every behavioral question ("Tell me about a time...") using this structure:

1.  **S - Situation:** Describe the context. Be brief.
2.  **T - Task:** What was your goal?
3.  **A - Action:** What did **YOU** do? (Use "I", not "We"). Focus on technical contributions, leadership, or conflict resolution.
4.  **R - Result:** What was the outcome? Use numbers (e.g., "Reduced latency by 20%", "Saved $10k").

## Top 5 Questions & Angles
1.  **"Tell me about a challenging project."**
    *   *Focus:* Technical depth, overcoming roadblocks.
2.  **"Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a teammate."**
    *   *Focus:* Empathy, professional communication, resolution.
3.  **"Tell me about a failure."**
    *   *Focus:* Self-reflection, learning, resilience.
4.  **"Why do you want to work here?"**
    *   *Focus:* Company mission, engineering culture, specific products.
5.  **"How do you handle tight deadlines?"**
    *   *Focus:* Prioritization, MVP definition, communication.
