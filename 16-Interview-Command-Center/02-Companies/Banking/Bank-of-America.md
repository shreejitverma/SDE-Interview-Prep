# Bank of America

## 🏢 Company Overview
**Industry:** Banking / Capital Markets | **HQ:** Charlotte, NC & NYC | **Remote:** Hybrid

### Mission & Values
Largest US bank by assets after JPMorgan. Global Markets division is a major revenue driver. Heavy investment in technology modernization and regulatory compliance (Basel III, FRTB).

### Engineering Culture
- Large enterprise technology org (~50,000+ technologists)
- Significant investment in Python modernization (moving from legacy Java/C# systems)
- Vendor management system (VMS) for contractors — Matlen Silver is the staffing agency
- Agile/Scrum teams within Global Markets Technology
- Heavy regulatory compliance focus — code must be audit-ready
- Multiple lines of business: Global Markets, Investment Banking, Wealth Management

### Tips for BofA Interviews
- **Contract role:** More practical/domain-focused than FAANG-style coding

- Expect questions about FRTB, market risk, derivatives, and Python implementation
- Demonstrate knowledge of regulatory capital calculations
