# 🏛️ Morgan Stanley

## 🏢 Company Overview
**Industry:** Global Investment Banking & Wealth Management | **HQ:** New York, NY (1585 Broadway) | **Remote:** Hybrid

### Division Spotlight: Fixed Income Division (FID) Strats & Alphawise
- **FID Strats (Quantitative Strategists):** Sit directly on the trading desks or closely aligned with traders. Responsible for quantitative pricing models, risk management, algorithmic execution, and electronic trading tools for fixed income, currencies, and commodities.
- **Alphawise (Research Division):** Morgan Stanley's proprietary big-data and alternative data research team, creating predictive signals and data-driven equity research.
- **Technology Focus:** Known historically for developing A+ (proprietary language), Scala, C++, and extensive modern Python for analytics and trade processing.
