# Google DeepMind
## 🏢 Overview
World's premier AI research lab. AlphaGo, AlphaFold, Gemini. Merged Google Brain + DeepMind in 2023. Research + productization.

## 📋 Interview Process
- Research Engineer: coding + ML theory + paper discussion + system design
- Expect deep understanding of transformers, attention, training dynamics
- They test: mathematical maturity, research taste, engineering ability
- Paper discussion round: they give you a paper to read and discuss
- Similar to Google SWE process but with ML depth

### Tips
- Read their recent papers before interviewing
- Be ready to discuss: Gemini, AlphaFold, scaling, safety
- Research engineer vs research scientist distinction matters
