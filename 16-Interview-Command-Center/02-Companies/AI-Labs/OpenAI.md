# OpenAI
## 🏢 Overview
Creator of GPT, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Codex. Leading commercial AI lab. ~$300B+ valuation. Mission: "Ensure AGI benefits all of humanity."

## 📋 Interview Process
- Recruiter screen → Technical phone → Onsite (4-5 rounds)
- Coding: LeetCode + ML implementation (implement attention from scratch)
- ML Systems: design serving infra, training pipelines
- Research/Theory: transformer depth, scaling, alignment
- Cultural: safety awareness, mission alignment
- They want engineers who understand the FULL stack from theory to production

### Tips
- Safety and alignment awareness is important — know their approach
- Be ready to discuss: scaling laws, emergent abilities, RLHF vs DPO
- Demonstrate ability to move fast and ship
