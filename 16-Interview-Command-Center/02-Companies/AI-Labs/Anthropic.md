## 🏢 Overview

## 📋 Interview Process

- Safety-first thinking is critical — know their approach

### Tips

- Know Constitutional AI paper
- Be ready to discuss: AI safety, alignment, responsible scaling
- They value "care about getting things right" over "move fast"
