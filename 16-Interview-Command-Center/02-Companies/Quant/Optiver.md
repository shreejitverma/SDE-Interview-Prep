# Optiver
## 🏢 Overview
Global market maker, one of the largest. Options market making specialist. Famous for mental math tests. Dutch culture — direct, pragmatic.

## 📋 Interview Process
1. **Online Assessment** — **Mental math test** (80 questions in 8 min) + coding
2. **Phone Screen** — C++ coding + probability
3. **Superday** — C++ systems, market making concepts, probability, mental math (again), behavioral
- **MENTAL MATH IS A GATE.** You must pass the speed test. Practice daily.
- They test market making intuition heavily
- C++ is expected at a systems level (memory, concurrency, networking)

### Mental Math Preparation
- Practice: https://arithmetic.zetamac.com
- Target: 80+ correct in 5 minutes (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)
- Practice daily for at least 2 weeks before applying
