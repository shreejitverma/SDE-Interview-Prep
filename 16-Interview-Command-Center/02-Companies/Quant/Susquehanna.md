# Susquehanna International Group (SIG)
## 🏢 Overview
Global quant trading firm. Known for options market making and poker culture. SIG teaches decision-making through game theory and poker. One of the largest options market makers.

## 📋 Interview Process
- Probability and game theory heavy
- **Poker game** — they literally play poker with candidates
- Mental math, probability, expected value calculations
- They look for: decision-making under uncertainty, risk assessment, quick thinking
- Less emphasis on coding compared to Citadel/Jane Street
