# Citadel

## 🏢 Overview
Two entities: **Citadel LLC** (hedge fund, $65B+ AUM) and **Citadel Securities** (market maker, ~25% of US equities volume). Founded by Ken Griffin.

### Culture
Extremely competitive, high-performance culture. Long hours expected. Compensation is top-tier. Strong emphasis on intellectual rigor.

## 📋 Interview Process

### Quant Developer
1. **HackerRank / Online Assessment** — C++ coding (90 min, 2-3 problems)
2. **Phone Screen** — C++ deep dive + probability
3. **Superday (4-6 rounds)** — C++ coding, system design (trading), probability, behavioral
- Heavy C++ focus: memory management, concurrency, performance
- They WILL ask about market microstructure

### Quant Researcher
1. **Online Assessment** — Math/probability test
2. **Phone Screen** — Brain teasers + probability
3. **Superday** — Probability, statistics, stochastic processes, mental math, coding (Python), market intuition

### Tips
- Citadel Securities interviews are more systems/low-latency focused
- Citadel LLC is more research/alpha focused
- They test C++ at a very deep level (templates, memory models, lock-free)
- Probability questions can be very hard (grad-school level)
- Know your market microstructure basics
