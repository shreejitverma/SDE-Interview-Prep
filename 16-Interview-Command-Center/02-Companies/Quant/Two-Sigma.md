# Two Sigma
## 🏢 Overview
Technology-driven hedge fund, $60B+ AUM. More tech-company-feel than traditional quant. Founded by David Siegel and John Overdeck. Strong ML/data science culture.

## 📋 Interview Process
### Quant Dev
1. Online Assessment (HackerRank)
2. 1-2 Phone Screens (coding + systems)
3. Onsite: coding (Python/C++), system design, probability, behavioral

### Quant Research
1. Online Assessment (math/coding)
2. Phone Screen (statistics, probability)
3. Onsite: statistical modeling, ML, brain teasers, coding, behavioral

### Tips
- More open to ML/data science approaches than pure math firms
- Python is acceptable (C++ preferred for latency-critical roles)
- Multiple phone screens before superday is common
- They value software engineering best practices even for research roles
