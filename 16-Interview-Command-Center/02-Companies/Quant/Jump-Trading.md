# Jump Trading
## 🏢 Overview
One of the most technically deep HFT firms. Heavy FPGA usage, kernel bypass, co-located systems. Known for pushing the boundaries of low-latency technology. Also active in crypto (Jump Crypto).

## 📋 Interview Process
- **Very technical.** Deep C++ systems knowledge expected.
- Network programming, kernel tuning, DPDK knowledge valued
- FPGA experience is a significant advantage
- Less behavioral than FAANG — almost all technical
- Brain teasers and probability for quant dev roles
- They test your ability to think about nanoseconds

### Tips
- Know your network stack cold (TCP internals, UDP multicast)
- Understand FPGA at a conceptual level even if you haven't coded one
- Practice C++ systems coding: allocators, lock-free, bit manipulation
- They want people who care about "the last nanosecond"
