# Jane Street

## 🏢 Overview
Proprietary trading firm. Known for using OCaml extensively. One of the most intellectually rigorous firms. ~$2000+ AUM. Famously secretive about strategies.

### Culture
Collaborative, intellectual, problem-solving obsessed. Very flat hierarchy. Everyone trades (even devs). Known for paying extremely well. Open floor plan, collaborative culture. "Jane Street is like a puzzle company that happens to trade."

## 📋 Interview Process

### Quant Trader / Researcher
1. **Phone Screen** — Probability puzzles, mental math (fast), market making game
2. **Superday** — Math (deep probability), trading games, mental math, behavioral
- **Mental math:** They test SPEED. Practice multiplication, estimation.
- **Trading games:** You'll play simulated markets. Focus on EV, information extraction.
- **Probability:** Very creative problems. Think carefully, don't rush.

### Software Developer
1. **Phone Screen** — Coding (OCaml preferred, but others accepted)
2. **Superday** — Coding, systems thinking, probability, behavioral
- OCaml knowledge is a major plus (not required)
- They test thinking process more than language mastery

### Tips
- Jane Street interviews are LEGENDARY for difficulty
- Math puzzles that look simple often have deep solutions
- They care about HOW you think, not just the answer
- Mental math speed matters — practice daily
- They genuinely enjoy teaching during interviews — engage!
- Know basic market making: bid-ask, inventory, adverse selection
