## 📋 Interview Process

### Onsite Breakdown (E5)
- **2× Coding:** LeetCode medium-hard, optimal solutions expected, sometimes 2 problems per round
- **1× System Design:** Practical (design Instagram feed, messenger, stories)
- **1× Behavioral:** "Move Fast" and "Impact" focused

### Tips & Known Patterns
- Coding rounds are FAST — often 2 problems in 45 min
- System design is more practical than Google (design a real product feature)
- They heavily test coding speed and accuracy
- "Why Meta?" — know their products, mention specific features
- E5+ requires strong system design signals
