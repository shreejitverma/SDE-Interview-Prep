# Amazon
## 🏢 Overview
**Culture:** 16 Leadership Principles dominate everything. Every interview answer must map to an LP.

## 📋 Interview Process
- **2× Coding:** LeetCode medium, clean code
- **1× System Design:** AWS-centric, scalable systems
- **2× Behavioral:** LP-focused (STAR method mandatory)
- **Bar Raiser:** One interviewer has veto power

### Amazon Leadership Principles for Interviews
| LP | Signal | Sample Q |
|----|--------|----------|
| Customer Obsession | Start with customer | "Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a customer" |
| Ownership | Long-term, never "not my job" | "Tell me about a time you took ownership beyond your role" |
| Dive Deep | Know details, audit | "Tell me about a time you had to really dig in to solve a problem" |
| Deliver Results | Outcomes matter | "Tell me about a time you delivered under a tight deadline" |
| Bias for Action | Speed, calculated risk | "Tell me about a time you took a risk" |
| Invent and Simplify | Innovation | "Tell me about an innovative solution you proposed" |
