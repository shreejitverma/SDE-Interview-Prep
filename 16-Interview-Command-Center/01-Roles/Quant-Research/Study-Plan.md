# 📋 Quant Research Study Plan — 10-Week Sprint

## Week 1-2: Probability Foundations
- [ ] Combinatorics: permutations, combinations, stars and bars, inclusion-exclusion
- [ ] Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, law of total probability
- [ ] Random variables: PMF, PDF, CDF, expectation, variance, covariance
- [ ] Common distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial, Geometric, Poisson, Uniform, Normal, Exponential
- [ ] **Daily:** 5 probability problems from "Green Book" or "Heard on the Street"

## Week 3-4: Advanced Probability & Stochastic Processes
- [ ] Markov chains: transition matrices, stationary distributions, first passage times
- [ ] Random walks: 1D, 2D, gambler's ruin, reflection principle
- [ ] Martingales: definition, optional stopping theorem, applications
- [ ] Generating functions: PGF, MGF, characteristic functions
- [ ] Central Limit Theorem, Law of Large Numbers
- [ ] **Daily:** 5 problems (increasing difficulty)

## Week 5-6: Brain Teasers & Mental Math
- [ ] Classic brain teasers: 100+ curated problems (Green Book, Heard on the Street, Glassdoor)
- [ ] Fermi estimation: order-of-magnitude calculations
- [ ] Mental math: multiplication tricks, percentage estimation, square roots
- [ ] Game theory basics: Nash equilibrium, minimax, auction theory
- [ ] **Daily:** 30 min mental math drills + 5 brain teasers

## Week 7-8: Statistics, ML & Coding
- [ ] Hypothesis testing, p-values, confidence intervals, power
- [ ] Regression: linear, logistic, regularization
- [ ] Time series: stationarity, autocorrelation, ARIMA basics
- [ ] Python coding: implement estimators, simulations, Monte Carlo
- [ ] **Daily:** 2-3 LeetCode (medium) + 1 statistical simulation

## Week 9-10: Trading Games & Mock Interviews
- [ ] Market making simulations: quoting, inventory risk, spreads
- [ ] Information games: auctions, value of information
- [ ] Pricing: options intuition, Black-Scholes, Greeks
- [ ] 3 full mock interviews
- [ ] Company-specific prep
