> **Key Intel:** This is the **LARGEST project** within this technology space, commanding **one-third (33%) of total annual funding**. It is a mission-critical, board-visible initiative.

### The Core Mandate:
1. **Vendor Platform Decommissioning:**
   - Retiring legacy third-party vendor surveillance platform (e.g., NICE Actimize / Nasdaq SMARTS).
   - Merging all detection rules, alert routing, and historical investigation functionality into Bank of America's proprietary **Global Supervisory Platform**.
2. **Cross-Line-of-Business (LOB) Expansion:**
   - Unifying surveillance across Rates, Equities, FX, Commodities, and Credit derivatives under a unified supervisory architecture.
3. **High-Volume Alert Processing & Frontend Work:**
   - Scaling backend pipelines to process millions of orders and trades daily.
   - Building intuitive, high-performance React/Angular user interfaces for compliance analysts to triage thousands of daily alerts without alert fatigue.
4. **Audit Remediation & Regulatory Work (SIA / IAI):**
   - Resolving **SIA** (Self-Identified Audit items) and **IAI** (Internal Audit Identified items) to guarantee zero open regulatory findings.
