## 🏛️ Bank of America Global Markets Technology Overview

### Institutional Scale
- **Total Assets:** Over $3.2 Trillion (2nd largest bank in the United States).
- **Global Markets Division:** Generates $18B+ in annual revenue, providing sales, trading, market making, and risk management across:
  - Fixed Income, Currencies, and Commodities (FICC)
  - Global Equities & Equity Derivatives
  - Global Rates & Credit Trading
- **Technology Scale:** 50,000+ technologists globally. BofA spends approximately $12 Billion annually on technology, with over $3.5B dedicated to new technology initiatives and modernization.

### Engineering Modernization Directives
1. **Python Modernization:** Global Markets has been aggressively migrating legacy monolithic systems (in C++ and Java) to modular, asynchronous Python microservices (FastAPI, Polars, modern scientific Python).
2. **Regulatory & Capital Modernization:** The FRTB (Fundamental Review of the Trading Book) program is a multi-year, board-visible initiative mandated by Basel III.1.
3. **Operational Excellence (OpEx):** Retiring expensive, black-box third-party vendor applications (e.g., NICE Actimize, Nasdaq SMARTS) and consolidating surveillance and supervisory capabilities into proprietary internal platforms.
