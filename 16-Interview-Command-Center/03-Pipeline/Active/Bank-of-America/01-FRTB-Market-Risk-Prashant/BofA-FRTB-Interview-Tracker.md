## 🎯 Role Responsibilities (From Job Specification)

### 1. Core Engineering & Architecture
- Design and build **scalable Python backend services, REST APIs, and microservices** using **FastAPI / Flask**.
- Deliver full-stack capabilities interfacing Python services with modern web frontends (**React / TypeScript / Angular**) for risk managers and traders.
- Write high-performance **SQL** and design relational schemas (**Oracle, PostgreSQL, SQL Server**) handling multi-gigabyte daily financial datasets.
- Ensure end-to-end data reconciliation, data quality lineage, and audit-ready test coverage.

### 2. FRTB & Market Risk Initiatives
- Support implementation of **Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB)** regulatory requirements (Basel III.1 / Basel IV).
- Develop computation engines for **FRTB Standardized Approach (SA)** and **Internal Models Approach (IMA)**.
- Implement calculations for **Sensitivities-Based Method (SBM)**: Delta, Vega, and Curvature across GIRR, CSR, Equity, FX, and Commodity risk classes.
- Model **Expected Shortfall (ES)**, **Stressed ES**, **Default Risk Charge (DRC)**, and **Non-Modellable Risk Factors (NMRF)**.
- Build automated pipelines reconciling trade positions, front-office sensitivities, P&L, and regulatory capital outputs.
- Investigate calculation breaks and discrepancies between trading-book risk metrics and regulatory capital filings.
