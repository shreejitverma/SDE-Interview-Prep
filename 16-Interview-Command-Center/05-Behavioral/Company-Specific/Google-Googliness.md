# 🟢 Google — Googliness & Leadership

> Google's behavioral round tests for "Googliness" (culture fit) and "General Cognitive Ability / Leadership."

---

## What Google Looks For

### Googliness
| Signal | What It Means | How to Show It |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Intellectual humility | You can be wrong and learn from it | "I realized my approach was wrong, so I..." |
| Conscientiousness | You do the right thing | "Even though it was harder, I chose to..." |
| Comfort with ambiguity | You thrive without clear directions | "The requirements were unclear, so I..." |
| Collaborative | You make the team better | "I helped my team by..." |
| Fun to work with | Positive energy, not arrogant | Be enthusiastic, show genuine curiosity |

### Leadership
| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| Emergent leadership | You lead when needed, even without title |
| Navigate ambiguity | You bring structure to chaos |
| Challenge status quo | You push for better, respectfully |
| Grow others | You mentor and teach |
