\passthrough{\lstinline!CACHE!} & 0 & Specialized inline cache entry

\passthrough{\lstinline!RESUME!} & 0 & Internal entry point for

\passthrough{\lstinline!LOAD\_CONST!} & const\_idx & Pushes
\passthrough{\lstinline!co\_consts[const\_idx]!} onto the stack. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!LOAD\_FAST!} & var\_num & Pushes local variable
\passthrough{\lstinline!var\_num!} onto the stack. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!STORE\_FAST!} & var\_num & Pops TOS and stores

\passthrough{\lstinline!co\_names[name\_idx]!} from global/builtin

\passthrough{\lstinline!BINARY\_OP!} & op\_id & Pops two items, performs

\passthrough{\lstinline!BUILD\_LIST!} & count & Pops
\passthrough{\lstinline!count!} items and pushes a new list. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!CALL!} & argc & Calls a function with
\passthrough{\lstinline!argc!} arguments. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!COMPARE\_OP!} & op\_id & Performs comparison

\passthrough{\lstinline!JUMP\_FORWARD!} & delta & Increments instruction
pointer by \passthrough{\lstinline!delta!}. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!POP\_JUMP\_IF\_FALSE!} & target & Pops TOS; if
false, jumps to \passthrough{\lstinline!target!}. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!RETURN\_VALUE!} & 0 & Returns TOS to the
caller. \\
\passthrough{\lstinline!YIELD\_VALUE!} & 0 & Yields TOS from a
