# Volume 00 - Foundations Under the Cloud

Volume 00 - Foundations Under the Cloud in the Cloud AI Engineering Zero to Godhood curriculum.

## Chapters

1. [Hypervisors, MicroVMs, and Sandboxing](Chapter_01_Hypervisors_MicroVMs_and_Sandboxing.md) - Nitro offload cards, Firecracker jailer and minimal devices, gVisor ptrace/KVM syscall interception, and Borg to Kubernetes lineage.
2. [Datacenter Networking Fabrics](Chapter_02_Datacenter_Networking_Fabrics.md) - Multi-stage Clos topologies, Google Jupiter fabric evolution, AWS datacenter networking, BGP peering, Anycast VIPs, and ECMP multipath hashing.
3. [DNS, Anycast, and Global Traffic Management](Chapter_03_DNS_Anycast_and_Global_Traffic_Management.md) - Route 53 and Cloud DNS internals, authoritative nameserver striping, latency-based routing, health probe fabrics, and DNSSEC validation.
4. [Edge Infrastructure and TLS Termination](Chapter_04_Edge_Infrastructure_and_TLS_Termination.md) - CloudFront and Google Cloud CDN points of presence, TLS 1.3 0-RTT handshakes, session ticket encryption, and hardware cryptographic offload.
5. [Storage Media and Block Storage Foundations](Chapter_05_Storage_Media_and_Block_Storage_Foundations.md) - NVMe flash endurance, write amplification, controller queue pairs, and physical attachment of network-attached block devices.
6. [Physical Failure Domains and Blast Radius](Chapter_06_Physical_Failure_Domains_and_Blast_Radius.md) - Datacenter power distribution, independent chilled-water loops, availability zone physical isolation, and regional backbones.
