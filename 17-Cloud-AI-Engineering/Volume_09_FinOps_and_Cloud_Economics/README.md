# Volume 09 - FinOps and Cloud Economics

Volume 09 - FinOps and Cloud Economics in the Cloud AI Engineering Zero to Godhood curriculum.

## Chapters

1. [Cloud Billing Models and Unit Cost Allocation](Chapter_01_Cloud_Billing_Models_and_Unit_Cost_Allocation.md) - Usage metering pipelines, cost allocation tags, GCP billing export to BigQuery, and unit metric definitions.
2. [Commitment Discounts: Savings Plans vs CUDs](Chapter_02_Commitment_Discounts_Savings_Plans_vs_CUDs.md) - AWS Compute/EC2 Savings Plans vs GCP Flexible/Compute Committed Use Discounts, breakeven formulas, and coverage sizing.
3. [Spot Economics and Interruption Handling](Chapter_03_Spot_Economics_and_Interruption_Handling.md) - Spot auction algorithms, two-minute and thirty-second interruption signals, checkpointing pipelines, and Spot fleet sizing.
4. [Network Egress Economics and Optimization](Chapter_04_Network_Egress_Economics_and_Optimization.md) - Inter-AZ, inter-region, and internet egress fee structures, Direct Connect / Interconnect economics, and cost avoidance.
5. [GPU and TPU Unit Economics](Chapter_05_GPU_and_TPU_Unit_Economics.md) - Hardware capitalization vs cloud hourly rental rates, TCO, cost per million tokens, and cost per training epoch.
6. [FinOps Organizational Practices and Automation](Chapter_06_FinOps_Organizational_Practices_and_Automation.md) - Showback, chargeback models, automated rightsizing lambdas, budget alerting thresholds, and anomalous spend detection.
