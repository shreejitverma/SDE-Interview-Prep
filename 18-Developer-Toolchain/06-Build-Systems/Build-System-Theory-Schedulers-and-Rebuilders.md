# Build System Theory: Schedulers, Rebuilders, and Dependency Graphs

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the formal mathematical and algorithmic taxonomy of build systems based on the seminal Mokhov, Mitchell, and Peyton Jones framework.
> Reaches Explain and Rebuild rungs by categorizing task dependencies, scheduling strategies, and rebuilding mechanisms.
> Establishes theoretical boundaries for comparing Make, Ninja, Shake, Bazel, and Buck2, while formalizing early cutoff and minimality guarantees.
