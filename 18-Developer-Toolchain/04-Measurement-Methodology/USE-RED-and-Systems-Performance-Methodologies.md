# USE, RED, and Systems Performance Methodologies

> [!summary] Scope
> This note owns holistic systems performance diagnostic methodologies formulated by Brendan Gregg and the SRE community.
> It covers the Use and Explain rungs by examining the Utilization, Saturation, and Errors (USE) method for resources alongside the Rate, Errors, and Duration (RED) method for services.
> It establishes structured investigation workflows that replace ad-hoc guessing during performance triage.
