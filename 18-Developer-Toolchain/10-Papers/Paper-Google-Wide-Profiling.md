# Paper: Google-Wide Profiling: Continuous Profiling for Data Centers (Ren et al. 2010)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the pioneering IEEE Micro 2010 paper on continuous, fleet-wide production profiling by Gang Ren et al.
> Dissects the architecture of hardware PMU interrupt sampling, randomized fleet collection daemons operating under low overhead, symbolization pipelines, and whole-datacenter hotspot analysis.
> Grounds modern continuous profiling systems including Parca, Pyroscope, and the OpenTelemetry profiling standard.
