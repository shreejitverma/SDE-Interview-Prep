# Paper: The Flame Graph (Gregg 2016)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns Brendan Gregg seminal 2016 Communications of the ACM paper on hierarchical performance visualization.
> Analyzes folded stack trace aggregation, explains why the horizontal axis represents population proportion rather than chronological execution time, and details differential flame graphs for comparative regression hunting.
> Grounds profiling visualization across all systems and application profilers.
