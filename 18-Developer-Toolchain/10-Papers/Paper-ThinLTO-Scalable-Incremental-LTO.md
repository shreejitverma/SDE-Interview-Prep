# Paper: ThinLTO: Scalable and Incremental Link-Time Optimization (Johnson et al. 2017)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the CGO 2017 compiler paper by Teresa Johnson, Mehdi Amini, and Xinliang David Li on scalable whole-program optimization in LLVM.
> Dissects the limitations of monolithic Link-Time Optimization, the construction of compact module summary indexes, global whole-program analysis, and parallel backend function importing with fine-grained caching.
> Grounds modern high-performance linking and compilation pipelines.
