# Paper: Simplifying and Isolating Failure-Inducing Input (Zeller and Hildebrandt 2002)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the formal analysis of automated test-case minimization and failure isolation by Andreas Zeller and Ralf Hildebrandt.
> Analyzes the ddmin divide-and-conquer algorithm, monotonicity and consistency assumptions, and experimental results on real-world bug reports.
> Grounds automated fault isolation tools including git bisect, creduce, cvise, and property-based test shrinking in Hypothesis.
