# Paper: Lessons from Building Static Analysis Tools at Google (Sadowski et al. 2018)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the CACM 2018 retrospective by Caitlin Sadowski et al.
> synthesizing a decade of lessons deploying static analysis across tens of thousands of engineers.
> Explains why standalone dashboard linters fail, establishes the strict false-positive threshold for developer trust, analyzes developer workflow integration during code review, and demonstrates the power of machine-applicable suggested fixes.
> Grounds organizational tooling strategy in the engineering systems pillar.
