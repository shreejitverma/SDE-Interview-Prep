# Paper: Reflections on Trusting Trust (Thompson 1984)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the seminal 1984 Turing Award lecture by Ken Thompson examining self-reproducing compiler backdoors and the limits of trust in source code.
> Analyzes the quine mechanism, the insertion of login and compiler trojans in binary compilers without leaving traces in source code, and David A.
> Wheeler Diverse Double-Compiling verification defense.
> Grounds the foundational threat model for the build systems and software supply chain pillars.
