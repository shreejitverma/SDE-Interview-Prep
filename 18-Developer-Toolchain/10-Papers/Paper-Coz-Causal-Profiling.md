# Paper: Coz: Finding Code that Counts with Causal Profiling (Curtsinger and Berger 2015)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the SOSP 2015 paper by Charlie Curtsinger and Emery D.
> Berger introducing causal profiling.
> Dissects the limitations of traditional profilers on concurrent applications, the virtual speedup mechanism achieved by pausing concurrent threads, and progress point throughput estimation.
> Grounds performance diagnosis by predicting the exact end-to-end system throughput impact of optimizing a specific line of code.
