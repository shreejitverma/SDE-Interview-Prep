# Paper: PubGrub and Minimal Version Selection (Weizenbaum 2018, Cox 2018)

> [!summary] Scope
> Owns the comparative analysis of modern software dependency resolution algorithms, contrasting Natalie Weizenbaum PubGrub specification with Russ Cox Minimal Version Selection.
> Dissects Conflict-Driven Clause Learning version solving with human-readable error derivation trees in PubGrub versus deterministic polynomial-time high-water-mark resolution in MVS.
> Grounds dependency resolution concepts and package management in the packaging pillar.
